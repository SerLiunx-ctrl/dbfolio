# 1.0.0-alpha.54

## 中文

- ER 表头支持再次点击收起详情，关闭按钮同样使用平滑的展开、收起动画。
- 拖动表头不会误开关详情；支持系统减少动态效果设置，收起期间立即退出键盘与辅助技术交互。AI 面板与详情仍可同时使用。

## English

- Click an ER table header again to collapse its details. Opening and closing, including the close button, now use a smooth transition.
- Dragging a header no longer toggles its details. Reduced-motion preferences are respected, and closing content immediately leaves keyboard and accessibility navigation. Details remain available alongside the AI panel.
