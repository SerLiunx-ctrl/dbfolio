# DBFolio 1.0.0-alpha.42

## 中文

- ER 关系图新增「AI 识别关联」：复用已有 AI 服务，选择表并补充业务说明，识别没有物理外键的潜在关联，支持复合字段和自引用。
- 候选显示字段配对、模型置信度、中文依据和核对提示，默认不勾选；采用前重新读取结构并拒绝过期、重复或无效映射。采用结果为本地业务标注，可统一保存/废弃，保留 AI 来源及理由。
- 仅发送用户选择的结构元数据，不读取数据行，不自动创建外键或执行 SQL。支持停止、后台任务与返回发起标签页，限制单次表/字段/候选规模；范围或要求变化后需重新识别。
- PostgreSQL 实库继续按用户要求待验收；本版不改变 R06 完整升级与锁屏识别问题的验收状态。

## English

- Added AI relationship discovery to ER diagrams. Reuse a configured AI service, select tables, and add business context to suggest potential relationships without physical foreign keys, including composite mappings and self-references.
- Review column pairs, model-assessed confidence, Chinese explanations, and verification notes. Candidates start unchecked. Adoption rereads the schema and rejects stale, duplicate, or invalid mappings. Accepted suggestions become local business annotations with AI provenance and rationale, using the existing save/discard workflow.
- Sends only explicitly selected schema metadata; does not read rows, create foreign keys, or execute SQL. Supports cancellation, background task tracking, and returning to the originating ER tab. Table, column, and candidate limits apply; changed scope or requirements require a new discovery run.
- Live PostgreSQL acceptance remains deferred at the user's request. This version does not change the outstanding acceptance status of complete upgrades or lock-state detection.
